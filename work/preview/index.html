Centrifugal pumps

From pump curve and suction conditions to seal and bearing health.

A centrifugal pump transfers energy from a rotating impeller to a liquid. The impeller adds velocity; the casing converts part of that velocity into pressure. The actual duty is where the pump curve meets the system curve.

01 · UNDERSTANDType, function & process service
02 · FINDCross-section, datasheet & curve
03 · MAINTAINInspect, trend & verify function

How to think through the equipment

Start with the pump tag and process duty. Check the approved datasheet and performance curve, then compare suction and discharge conditions with the intended operating point. Assess the whole train: driver, coupling, base, piping, bearings, lubrication and seal support.

Field signals worth recording

  • Flow, suction and discharge pressure, motor current, vibration and bearing temperature trends.
  • Seal leakage, unusual noise, lubrication condition, alignment and pipe strain observations.
  • Compare findings with baseline, operating limits, equipment history and OEM guidance.

Important notes

Safety first. Verify isolation, depressurisation, draining, stored energy controls and PTW/LOTO requirements before intrusive work.
NPSH margin matters. Compare NPSHA with the manufacturer’s NPSHR curve and site criteria. Do not infer NPSHR from an equation.
Use evidence. A failed bearing or seal is a symptom; confirm the failure mechanism before assigning root cause.

Competency checkpoints

→Identify pump type, installed components, service and normal operating envelope.
→Read the cross-section, datasheet, nameplate and performance curve.
→Recognise cavitation, seal, bearing, lubrication, alignment and pipe-strain indicators.
→Plan work safely, capture inspection evidence, verify the return to service and prevent recurrence.

Fastener identification in the field

First distinguish a hex-head bolt or nut from a socket-head screw. Check the marked thread and pitch or approved drawing. Fully seat the correct tool; if it rocks or engages only the corners, stop and re-check. Keep metric and imperial tooling separate.

1Identify fastener head style and likely standard.
2Confirm nominal diameter, pitch and material grade as required.
3Use the approved torque value, sequence and calibrated tool for critical joints.

Important maintenance notes

Apply site PTW/LOTO and verify safe isolation before loosening equipment fasteners. For pressure, chemical, hot or rotating service, follow the equipment procedure.
